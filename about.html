About the World Music Scales Atlas

The World Music Scales Atlas is an independent digital research and educational platform for exploring musical pitch systems across cultures. It combines source-linked ethnomusicological documentation, computational pitch analysis and interactive geographic visualization.

Research and methodology

Explore measured tuning inventories, theoretical modal models and nominal instrumental pitch collections. Intervals are expressed in cents, with octave-based models and non-octave spans represented separately. The Atlas supports transposition, synthesized pitch playback and comparative visualization.

Each model identifies its tradition, evidence type and source where available. Numerical similarity does not establish cultural equivalence; the Atlas does not claim to reproduce complete performance practice or definitive cultural intonation. Consult the reference library and methodology and guide.
