About the Atlas

The World Music Scales Atlas is an independent interactive digital platform dedicated to the exploration, visualization, and comparative understanding of musical scales, modal systems, and musical traditions from around the world. It integrates music theory, cultural knowledge, geographic visualization, and interactive technology within a unified educational and exploratory environment.

Creator and Developer of the World Music Scales Atlas

This application was independently conceived, designed, and developed by Dr. Pouya Hosseini as part of a broader collection of interactive digital applications dedicated to music theory, musical heritage, and computational music exploration.
