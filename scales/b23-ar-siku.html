Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Reported pitch names and compass encoded as semitone distances.

López describes a 13-tube family with interleaved Arka and Ira rows, a G-major pitch collection, and a lowest D and highest B. This 12-TET reference starts at the lowest tube and follows the written pitch pattern. The field study also describes microtonal differences across siku families.

Instrument pitch facts only. No source figures, recordings or performances reproduced.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
