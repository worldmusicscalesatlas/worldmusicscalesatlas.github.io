Evidence and interpretation

Documented pitch representation rechecked

Independent mathematical encoding of source-described pitches or interval ideal; no measured intonation implied.

The collection identifies a modern professional notch flute marked Walata G. originario, likely from the La Paz area, with an equal-tempered G-major tuning and G4–G6 range. The nominal diatonic pitches are encoded across the described compass; the model is not a measurement of this flute, nor a representation of community-specific Aymara or Quechua ceremonial qina. Chromatic fingerings are outside the model.
