Evidence and interpretation

English and Vietnamese indexed museum texts agree on all six note names; direct page fetch failed. Cents computed from the named pitches; no acoustic measurement claimed.

One basic bridge-tuning example for the đàn tranh documented by the Vietnamese Institute of Musicology in its Huế chamber orchestra description. The Western solfège spelling is a nominal reference only; variable intonation, ornaments, string pressure and melodic hierarchy are not represented. The English and Vietnamese descriptions are versions of the same source, not independent measurements.
