Evidence and interpretation

Published teaching tuning checked

Public educational references distinguish the reentrant high-G form from the low-G form; notes converted to semitone distances.

Publicly taught contemporary ukulele tuning. String pitches are placed in ascending order for the Atlas; string order, chord voicings and fretted compass are not represented. This is not a traditional Native Hawaiian chant scale, Indigenous ceremonial knowledge or an assertion of community permission.

Publicly documented abstract pitch facts only; no source figures, recordings, songs, or protected cultural expression reproduced. No claim of cultural endorsement.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
