Existing published model retained; this audit validates its data integrity, not the underlying musical or acoustic claim.

Widely discussed idealization, not a claim that Thai musicians actually tune all pitches equally. Compare it with the unequal source-specific instrumental examples. The first pitch is an arbitrary playback reference, not a demonstrated musical tonic. The pitch inventory does not imply a performed modal scale.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
