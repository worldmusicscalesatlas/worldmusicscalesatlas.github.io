Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Researcher's published pitch names checked directly and encoded as semitone distances.

Arja Kastinen reports the tunings used on two 2014 replicas of an instrument marked 1830 from the Rovaniemi area. These are modern experimental nominal tunings; not measurements of the historic original or a claim about a fixed Finnish folk scale. No octave closure added.
