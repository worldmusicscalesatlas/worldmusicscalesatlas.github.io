Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Scope and limitations are stated in the model description; observed spans are not normalized.

One tuning of Kufuna Kandonga’s eight-lamella likembe, sorted by ascending pitch. Both near-unison lamellae are retained separately, in contrast to averaging in the secondary compilation. Five observations concern one musician and demonstrate tuning variability, not five generic Angolan scales. The last two measured pitches are intentionally close in the player's practice; eight sounding pitches do not imply eight modal degrees.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
