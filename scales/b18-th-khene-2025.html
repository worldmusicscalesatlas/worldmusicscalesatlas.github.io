Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Independent calculation from stated fundamentals; two conflicting source numbers resolved by the reported FFT peak and harmonic fit. HTML and PDF text checked; rendered pages inaccessible.

All sixteen separately measured reeds sorted by ascending frequency; both G4 reeds (384 and 395 Hz) are retained. Right tube 8 uses 887 Hz, as stated in the abstract, results and harmonic fit; the 493-Hz peak listed first in Table 1 is not consistent with that harmonic series. Left tube 4 uses 331 Hz from Table 2, the plotted first peak and regression fit, rather than the 329-Hz figure in the prose. Cumulative cents are rounded to the nearest cent. The measurement represents an Isan-style khene, not a measured instrument from Laos; the regional marker does not assert the specimen's precise collection location. This is a pitch inventory, not the five lai modes or the full drone/melody system.

Publicly documented instrument tuning or teaching information; no songs, recordings, or restricted cultural materials reproduced. No claim of community endorsement or authorization for wider reuse.
