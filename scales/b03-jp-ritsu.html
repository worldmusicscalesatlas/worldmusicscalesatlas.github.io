Evidence and interpretation

Structural check passed; source-level recheck pending

Existing published model retained; this audit validates its data integrity, not the underlying musical or acoustic claim.

A constructed octave species: two identical 500-cent tetrachords separated by a 200-cent step. Western semitone notation is a mathematical encoding, not measured intonation. Nuclear tones and melodic functions are not represented by the interval ring. Reference pitch is not a claim of a unique tonic.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
