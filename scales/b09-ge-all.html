Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Scope and limitations are stated in the model description; observed spans are not normalized.

Aligned melodic pitch-distribution centers for selected eight consecutive degrees of the 1966 Erkomaishvili multitracked chant corpus. Corpus statistics are not a universally fixed Georgian scale. The full distribution beyond this range and harmonic estimates are not represented. Only independently computed pitch intervals are used; audio and figures are not reproduced.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
