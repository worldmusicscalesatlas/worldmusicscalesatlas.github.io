Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Primary text of Partitura 61 cross-checked against the instrument identification, bar count and first interval in p. 165 prose. Rendered notation was not accessible; full visual verification remains pending.

Individual marimba built in the Telembí tradition of Esmeraldas. Adjacent cent values are retained as printed; the number of bars and first interval agree with the author's independent prose on p. 165. The source notes that instruments were built at sea level but recorded at 2,800 m in Quito, which may affect tuning. No normalization or octave closure. One instrument rather than a performed mode. The fourth comparative row (Guillermo Ayoví) is withheld because the extracted interval count is inconsistent with the reported number of bars.

Publicly documented instrument tuning or teaching information; no songs, recordings, or restricted cultural materials reproduced. No claim of community endorsement or authorization for wider reuse.
