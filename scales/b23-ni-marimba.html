Evidence and interpretation

Documented pitch representation rechecked

Source-specific pitch representation; not a claim of national or universal temperament.

The collection describes this 22-key specimen's approximately Mixolydian B-flat3–B-flat6 compass. The nominal three-octave inventory is reconstructed from that stated pitch pattern and count; individual bar frequencies are not provided. This is not a measured tuning or a representation of the dance repertoire. The instrument also has religious and secular folkloric contexts; no ceremonial musical material is reproduced.

Publicly reported pitch facts or statistical aggregates only. No recordings, song transcriptions, diagrams or ceremonial material reproduced. No claim of wider community endorsement.
