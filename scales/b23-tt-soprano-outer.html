Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Original frequency-column text and chapter methodology checked; page facsimile unavailable. Mass-loading bias of 1–3 Hz reported by the author, not corrected without note-specific estimates.

Twelve fundamental-mode frequencies measured on the paint-coated fourths-and-fifths soprano steelpan supplied by maker Aubrey Bryan. Only the outer C4–B4 register is modeled; upper-register data and higher vibrational modes are excluded. Cumulative pitch positions were converted using 1200 log2(f/f0) and rounded to the nearest cent. The accelerometer lowered frequencies by 1–3 Hz according to the source, so these values are laboratory modal estimates, not corrected free-radiation pitches. No octave closure is added. Geographic association is the Trinidadian instrument tradition; the specimen's manufacturing location is not inferred.

Publicly reported pitch facts or statistical aggregates only. No recordings, song transcriptions, diagrams or ceremonial material reproduced. No claim of wider community endorsement.
