Evidence and interpretation

Primary numerical evidence rechecked

Direct recalculation from the frequency column and independent comparison with the secondary interval transcription; original table text accessible, facsimile not independently obtained. Approximate measurements, not fixed temperament.

One flute acquired and acoustically examined by the researcher. Cumulative pitch positions were recalculated from reported integer-Hz peaks using 1200 log2(f/f0), rounded to the nearest cent, and differ by at most one cent per step from the secondary DaMuSc transcription. These are sample-dependent peak pitches, not precise fixed performance intonation; blowing and melodic context can change the pitch. The full measured register is retained, no octave closure or unmeasured pitches are inserted. The initial V degree is the playback reference rather than a claimed tonic. This regional Brazilian banda cabaçal tradition is not assigned to a specific Indigenous nation.

Publicly reported instrument measurements; no songs, recordings, or ceremonial knowledge reproduced. No claim of community endorsement or authorization for wider reuse.
