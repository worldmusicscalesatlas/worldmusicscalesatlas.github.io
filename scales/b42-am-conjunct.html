Evidence and interpretation

Published theoretical construction checked

Two accessible explanatory sources agree on linked tetrachords. Numerical construction calculated independently; no source figure copied.

The published theoretical account describes a succession of conjunct major tetrachords, with the last degree of one shared by the next. Three repetitions are an Atlas illustration of the documented construction, not a claim that the system ends at 1500 cents. Different modal centers, tetrachord alterations, melodic function and historical non-tempered intonation are outside this model.

Publicly documented abstract pitch facts only; no source figures, recordings, songs, or protected cultural expression reproduced. No claim of cultural endorsement.
