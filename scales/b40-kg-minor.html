Evidence and interpretation

Primary theoretical pitch labels checked

Chapter 4.1 text checked; the explicit ascending order of the reported note set is encoded, with no octave closure.

Sipos identifies a lower fifth-related pitch and a higher trichord/tetrachord region in older Kyrgyz folk-song strata. This representation includes the optional upper pitch in parentheses. The source explicitly describes instability of the third and other scale degrees; the nominal semitone model is not a measurement, a fixed tonic assertion or a song transcription.

Publicly documented abstract pitch facts only; no source figures, recordings, songs, or protected cultural expression reproduced. No claim of cultural endorsement.
