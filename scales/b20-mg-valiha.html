Evidence and interpretation

Documented pitch representation rechecked

Independent mathematical encoding of source-described pitches or interval ideal; no measured intonation implied.

A particular 15-string metal-string bamboo tube zither made for the tourist market. The published left and right hand note sets combine into a B-flat major collection spanning B-flat4–B-flat6. This encoding preserves the 15 nominal sounding pitches rather than reducing the instrument to a generic octave. No claim of measured frequencies, universal Malagasy tuning, or ritual music.
