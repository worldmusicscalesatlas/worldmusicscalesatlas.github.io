Evidence and interpretation

Published nominal pitch description checked

Text states compass, collection and pipe count; all three agree with the independently enumerated pitch inventory. Rendered dissertation page unavailable; no visual verification claimed.

The dissertation specifies twenty pipes and a B3 to G6 compass in the G-major pitch collection. The whole nominal compass is retained; not a measured tuning or a universal Romanian folk scale. Alternate instrument tunings and lip-induced pitch changes are outside this reference.

Publicly documented abstract pitch facts only; no source figures, recordings, songs, or protected cultural expression reproduced. No claim of cultural endorsement.
