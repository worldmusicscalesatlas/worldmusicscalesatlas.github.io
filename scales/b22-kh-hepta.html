Evidence and interpretation

Documented pitch representation rechecked

Both pitch spellings and interval descriptions agree in the primary publication's prose on p. 9. PDF text accessed; page-image retrieval failed.

The author gives a Western-note approximation of structural pitch organization, with G as reference in pinn peat. The pentatonic representation selects five structural pitches; the heptatonic representation includes all seven. Individual performers and instruments differ, and the author explicitly disputes universal equidistance. Neither representation specifies actual modal finals, ornaments or exact performed intervals.

Public educational or scholarly instrument/pitch facts only; original prose, audio, images, songs and ceremonial materials not reproduced. No claim of community endorsement or authorization for broader reuse.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
