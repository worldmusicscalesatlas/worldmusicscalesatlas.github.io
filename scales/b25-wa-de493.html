Museum-listed frequencies converted using 1200 × log₂ of each successive frequency ratio. Playback transposes the interval sequence.

Six contiguous bars in the upper part of the 18-bar specimen. Where two peaks are reported for these bars, the lower peak corresponds to the catalogued approximate pitch.
