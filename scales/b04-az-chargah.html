Evidence and interpretation

Structural check passed; source-level recheck pending

Existing published model retained; this audit validates its data integrity, not the underlying musical or acoustic claim.

The full ascending pitch range is reconstructed from the tetrachord combination described by Hajibeyov. A 100-cent semitone is an encoding convention, not a measured Azerbaijani temperament; actual thirds and semitones can differ. The lowest pitch is the playback reference. In the source the modal tonic is the fourth pitch, 500 cents above that reference in this encoding. The pitch sequence does not encode modal functions, ornamentation or the complete muğam performance.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
