Evidence and interpretation

Primary theoretical pitch labels checked

The published analytical description and solmization conventions checked directly. No additional notes invented.

The source reports la–ti–do–re–mi–(fa)–sol–la, with the sixth degree optional in some broader-range tunes. This theoretical comparator includes that degree. This is Sipos's generalized analytical structure, not an actual recorded intonation, individual melody or a national scale.

Publicly documented abstract pitch facts only; no source figures, recordings, songs, or protected cultural expression reproduced. No claim of cultural endorsement.

A numerical pitch pattern is not a complete account of a musical tradition, performance practice or melodic function. Equal interval patterns do not establish cultural equivalence.
