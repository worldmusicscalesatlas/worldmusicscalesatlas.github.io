Evidence and interpretation

Published teaching tuning checked

Two independent public teaching references agree on pitch names and reentrance. No absolute historical octave asserted.

The four string courses are conventionally tuned in the order A–D–F♯–B with the B below the F♯. Sorted sounding pitch order is A–B–D–F♯. These are nominal open strings and not a melodic scale or a statement of historical intonation.
