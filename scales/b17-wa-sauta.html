Evidence and interpretation

Modern teaching representation rechecked

Three public teaching sources agree on the modern pitch collection. Traditional microtonal numbers remain under review.

Modern equal-tempered teaching version intended for playing alongside other fixed-pitch instruments; independently encoded from the published note names. F-major with B-flat raised to B-natural. Not a measured traditional Mandé tuning; historical Silaba, Sauta and other tunings vary with player and region. No claim that Western pitch geometry captures the modal repertoire.

Publicly documented instrument tuning or teaching information; no songs, recordings, or restricted cultural materials reproduced. No claim of community endorsement or authorization for wider reuse.
