Independent mathematical encoding of source-described pitches or interval ideal; no measured intonation implied.

The educational collection describes the 20-bar specimen as approximately equal-stepped. This is the exact seven-step mathematical ideal used as a comparator, not a reconstruction of its individual bars, a claim of exact tuning, or a representation of hereditary jeli repertoire. Actual bar frequencies and full measured span are not supplied.
