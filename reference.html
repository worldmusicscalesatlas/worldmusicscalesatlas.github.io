World music scales: reference library

Explore 393 documented public tuning models and 7 Western diatonic modes. Each entry provides the ordered numerical intervals in cents, cultural context, tuning method and available references. This library complements the interactive globe and comparison tools of the World Music Scales Atlas.

Models can represent theoretical approximations, historical ratios, measured pitch inventories or statistical estimates. They should not be treated as definitive descriptions of performance intonation or cultural identity. Evidence limitations are stated on each model page.
